MISSING-VALUE HANDLING
- Missing 2025 destination visit values are retained as blank/NaN in the master dataset.
- A blank means the destination was not listed in the supplied Top 20/Top 10 table; it does NOT mean zero visitors.
- Google Trends values are summarised for description only, not used as cross-destination K-Means features.
- District-level arrival figures are retained as context only, not used as destination-level K-Means features.
- Statewide accommodation totals are not assigned to individual destinations.

LIMITATIONS
- Accommodation counts are counts of entries in supplied Kerala Tourism PDFs, not a verified census of all operating businesses.
- Restaurant and cafe counts depend on the supplied source and collection method; verify source/date in the report.
- Only 10 destination observations are available, so K-Means results will be exploratory and sensitive to feature choice.
- Adventure business/activity counts were not available and are excluded from clustering.
